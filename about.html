About PumpReport

PumpReport covers crypto momentum, token launches, whale moves, and market culture — reported fast, with context. We write about the price action, the launchpad mechanics, the whale trades, and the internet culture around crypto markets as it happens, not as a lagging recap.

Every figure we publish is attributed to a named source at the time of publication. We don't fabricate prices, market caps, trading volumes, or projections, and we don't predict where any token is headed. Where a story involves genuine risk — a new token launch, a leveraged trade, a prediction market — we say so plainly, because most of what we cover is speculative by nature.

PumpReport does not carry sponsored or brand content until a story organically qualifies for coverage on its own merits. We're an independent media property and are not affiliated with any token issuer, exchange, or platform we report on. Where we do have a business relationship with something we're covering, we disclose it — see our Editorial Policy for how that works in practice.

PumpReport is part of a small network of independent publications.

Nothing on this site is financial advice. Crypto markets, and the assets we cover most closely, are highly volatile and speculative — always do your own research and consult a qualified financial advisor before making investment decisions.
