About FrontierTech.news

FrontierTech.news covers emerging technology for builders and early adopters — AI, blockchain, energy, biotech, and space. Our editorial approach is simple: explain what's actually changing, why it matters, and skip the hype.

AI is our heaviest coverage area because it's what's moving fastest right now, but we cover it alongside the technologies converging with it — clean energy scaling to meet AI's power demand, biotech accelerating on AI-assisted discovery, and the blockchain and space stories that round out what "emerging tech" actually means in 2026.

Editorial independence

FrontierTech.news is part of a small network of independent publications. Each site is edited and reported on its own terms. Where we cover a company we have a business relationship with, we disclose it plainly and hold the coverage to the same evidentiary bar as everything else we publish — forward-looking claims about unproven capabilities are described as forward-looking, not as fact.
