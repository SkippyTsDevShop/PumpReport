The Solana Launchpad Wars: How StonkFun Briefly Out-Earned Pump.fun

A launchpad that pairs meme tokens against tokenized stocks instead of SOL had its one big day against the market leader — and helped push Solana's token-launch count to an all-time record in the process.

Published Sept 21, 2026 · 6 min read · Launches Desk

On September 6, 2026, a Solana launchpad called StonkFun generated $1,508,195 in protocol revenue in a single day — enough to temporarily surpass pump.fun, the platform that has defined the meme coin launchpad category since Solana's meme boom began. It didn't last: over the trailing 30 days from August 9 to September 7, pump.fun still commanded 64.2% of three-way launchpad revenue share, against StonkFun's 7.58%, producing roughly 8.5 times StonkFun's total fee volume. But a single day of real competition against the category leader is still a real event, and the mechanic behind it is genuinely new.

Tokens paired against stocks, not SOL

Every meme coin launchpad before this one worked the same way: you launch a token, it's paired against SOL or a stablecoin, and its price is purely a function of how much people want to buy that token. StonkFun changes the pairing. Users launch tokens paired against tokenized equities instead — its own native STONK token is paired with SPYx, a Backed Finance product that tracks the S&P 500. That creates a dual-value dynamic: a StonkFun token's price reflects both speculative demand for the token itself and the performance of the equity it's paired against, so in theory (though not always in practice, as covered below) it inherits some of the underlying stock's price behavior along with the usual meme coin volatility.

The platform also runs a buyback mechanism: a portion of trading fees from its v3 liquidity pools funds purchases and burns of the platform's largest tokens, with cumulative STONK buybacks exceeding $1 million so far.

The record it helped produce

StonkFun's mechanic — along with copycats and competing platforms like Robinhood's Pons launchpad — is part of what drove Solana to an all-time record of 263,000 new token launches in a single day on September 10, 2026, according to Solscan data reported by Cryptopolitan. The article frames this as Solana "expanding beyond meme coins": the new wave includes "reflection tokens" paired not just against stocks but against RWA perpetual futures, other tokens, NFTs, and blue-chip crypto assets. Raydium, the DEX where much of this trading settles, saw a corresponding jump in volume, and Meteora — a DEX enabling trades between any two Solana assets rather than just against stablecoins — became part of the plumbing making these unconventional pairings tradeable at all.

Two tokens got singled out as high performers in this wave: STONK itself, and ZCAT, a token reflecting Zcash's ZEC. Both are covered in more depth in our companion piece on reflection tokens' boom-and-bust volatility.

What one good day actually proves

A single day of out-earning the market leader is a headline, not a changing of the guard — pump.fun's 30-day dominance makes that clear. What it does prove is that Solana's launchpad market isn't settled: when a new pairing mechanic offers something genuinely different (tradeable exposure to a stock index through a meme-coin wrapper, in this case), capital moves toward it fast, even if only for a day. As one report on the space put it, this is what it looks like when "speculative capital moves down the curve when the majors go quiet" — a description that will likely apply to whatever launchpad tries the next new pairing mechanic, too.
