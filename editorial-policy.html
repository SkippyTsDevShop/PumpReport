Editorial Policy

PumpReport covers fast-moving, speculative markets. That makes editorial standards more important, not less. Here's how we operate.

Sourcing

Every factual claim in a PumpReport article is drawn from named, linked sources — on-chain data, exchange and protocol data, regulatory filings, and reporting from other outlets we credit explicitly. We do not fabricate prices, market capitalizations, trading volumes, or projections. Where figures differ between sources, or where a number is time-sensitive, we say so in the article rather than picking whichever number is more dramatic.

No investment advice

PumpReport never recommends buying, selling, or holding any specific asset. We explain what happened and how the mechanics work; what you do with that information is your decision. Every article touching a tradeable asset carries a disclosure noting this.

Editorial independence

Coverage decisions — what we report on and how — are made by the editorial desk, not by advertisers, partners, or sponsors. PumpReport does not run sponsored or brand content until a story qualifies for coverage on its own editorial merits; we don't cover a project because it's connected to our network, and we don't avoid covering a project's failures because it is.

Disclosure

Where PumpReport or its network has a business relationship with a company, token, or platform mentioned in an article, that relationship is disclosed directly in the piece. If we're ever wrong about something, we correct it and note the correction.

Corrections

If you spot an error, factual or otherwise, in something we've published, email us at pumpreport@proton.me and we'll look into it. Material corrections are noted on the affected article.

Questions

For anything not covered here, reach the editorial desk at pumpreport@proton.me.
